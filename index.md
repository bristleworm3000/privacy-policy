# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: May 2, 2025_

**AMAdder** ("the App") respects your privacy. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does **not collect, store, or share any personal information**. We do not request, store, or transmit your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal data.

## 2. Apple Music Access

The App integrates with **Apple Music** using Apple's official MusicKit framework. This allows the App to:

- Search for songs in the Apple Music catalog,
- Access your Apple Music playlists,
- Open songs in the Apple Music app.

**Important:**
- All Apple Music data is accessed **locally on your device only.**
- No Apple Music account data or playlists are collected, stored, or transmitted to our servers (we don’t have any servers).

## 3. Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## 4. Security

As no data is collected, there are no specific security measures related to data storage. All interactions with Apple Music are handled securely through Apple's APIs.

## 5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the App or on our website.
